- 2026-09-22: The tariff Excel/CSV source is a direct-lookup table (no per-TSO calculation formulas) — each row is keyed by TSO, network point, direction (entry/exit), product type, and validity period, and carries a ready-to-use price, currency, and unit. Decided by: user.
- 2026-09-22: Source tariff rows mix currencies and units (e.g., HUF/kWh/h, EUR/MWh/d). The Route Tariff Calculator (tab 1) always converts results to EUR/MWh using a hardcoded FX-rate constant in config (no live FX API for this term); the Capacity Fee Calculator (tab 2) shows costs in the row's native currency/unit with no conversion. Decided by: user.
- 2026-09-22: Route Tariff Calculator optimizer algorithm: for each calendar month touching the requested booking period, independently compare the monthly product's price against the sum of that month's daily product prices and keep the cheaper; partial edge months not fully covered by the period are always priced day-by-day; monthly products always align to full calendar months (no rolling windows, no cross-month interaction). Decided by: user.
- 2026-09-22: Capacity Fee Calculator's monthly invoice breakdown splits the selected product instrument's total cost across the months it spans, proportionally to each month's calendar-day count. Decided by: user.
- 2026-09-22: Network point selection in both tabs must also capture direction (entry/exit), since capacity tariffs differ by flow direction at a given point. Decided by: user.
- 2026-09-25: MVP scope narrowed to FGSZ (Hungarian TSO) network points only, using the uploaded export `DM_Tariff_capacity_fee_20260924123613.xlsx` (sheet `Capacity_fee`); the README's "3 TSOs" goal is deferred beyond the MVP. Decided by: user.
- 2026-09-25: In the FGSZ-only MVP a "route" (Route Tariff Calculator, tab 1) is one FGSZ entry point plus one FGSZ exit point inside Hungary, and the route tariff is the sum of the entry tariff and the exit tariff for the same booking period. Decided by: user.

- 2026-09-25: Price-cell semantics: each FGSZ price cell (Year, Q*, M_*, D_*) is the HUF price for booking 1 kWh/h of capacity for the whole product period; cost = capacity in kWh/h x cell price. Tab 2 converts the user's kWh/d input to kWh/h by dividing by 24. Decided by: user (on Claude's recommendation).
- 2026-09-25: Tab 1's EUR/MWh figure = total booking cost for the route and period, divided by the energy the booked capacity could carry in that period (1 kWh/h x 24 h x days in the period), converted HUF to EUR with the fixed FX constant; it assumes 100% utilisation. Decided by: user (on Claude's recommendation).
- 2026-09-25: The Route Tariff Calculator optimizer now also considers quarterly products (calendar quarters Oct-Dec, Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep): where the booking period fully covers a calendar quarter, the quarterly price is compared with its three monthly prices and the cheaper is kept. This refines the 2026-09-22 rule that each month is decided independently; yearly products stay deferred. Reason: in the FGSZ data monthly always beats the sum of daily prices, while quarterly beats its three months in 65% of cells. Decided by: user (on Claude's recommendation).
- 2026-09-25: The Capacity Fee Calculator (tab 2) offers four product instruments: gas year (Oct 1-Sep 30), quarter, month, and day; within-day is excluded. Each selection maps to exactly one price cell (Year, Q*, M_*, D_*), cost = capacity in kWh/h x that cell's price, and the monthly invoice breakdown splits by calendar days (gas year -> 12 months, quarter -> 3, month -> 1, day -> 1). Yearly is available on tab 2 only; the tab 1 optimizer still excludes it. Decided by: user (on Claude's recommendation).
