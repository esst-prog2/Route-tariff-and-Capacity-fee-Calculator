- 2026-10-01: Opened a spike in response to the teacher's review (the optimiser never buys a product longer than the request, so 2026-10-02..2026-12-30 costs 3040.5 against 2052.1 for the whole of Q4, and 11-02..11-28 costs 1012.1 against 819.2 for plain November, while the README and app say "cheapest combination"). Question: "Across realistic bookings, how much does exact-cover pricing lose against allowing over-booking?" What counts as an answer: two numbers, the median and the worst-case overpay in percent, where overpay = (optimiser total − cheapest over-covering plan) / cheapest over-covering plan × 100, measured over about thirty realistic booking periods on each of the thirty-five entry×exit point pairs; the over-covering plan may buy any quarter, month or day that touches the period, and over-booking is used only when it is the cheapest option. The spike is a throwaway script; the app is not changed. Decided by: user.
- 2026-10-01: Spike answer (synthetic sample `data/sample/tariffs_sample.xlsx`): median overpay 0.0 %, worst-case overpay 135.8 % (Mosonmagyaróvár AT>HU -> Csanádpalota HU>RO, 2026-12-02..2026-12-31: 1767.2 against 749.6 HUF per kWh/h for plain December). 428 of the 1050 cases overpay, and their median is 35.3 %; the median of 0.0 % comes from the 12 period shapes (exact months/quarters, short in-month bookings) where exact cover is already optimal, so it depends on the period mix. Periods that miss a few edge days of a month or quarter are the worst: per-period medians of 31-86 % and worst cases of 78-136 %. The script reproduces both review figures exactly (3040.5 vs 2052.1 = 48.2 %; 1012.1 vs 819.2 = 23.6 %). Decided by: Claude (measured).
- 2026-10-01: How the spike was measured: a throwaway script outside the repo (no app change) took 30 hand-picked periods (the two from the review, months and quarters missing a few edge days, exact months and quarters, single day, weeks, balance-of-month, multi-month blocks with ragged edges, three across the 1 October gas-year boundary, one in the previous gas year) on all 35 entry×exit pairs (1050 cases; the 4 same-EIC pairs the app rejects give the same median and worst, 0.0 % and 135.8 %). Exact = `core.optimizer.optimize(...).total`. Over-cover = the cheapest set of quarters, months and days that covers every booked day, possibly more: for each month touched, min(monthly price, day prices of the booked days), then for each quarter touched, min(quarterly price, sum of those month costs); exact because the calendar products nest. Both sides use the app's `RouteOracle` (each product priced from the row valid on its first day); yearly products were left out, as in the app. Overpay % = (exact − over-cover) / over-cover × 100. Choice of periods, product set and formula: Decided by: Claude.
