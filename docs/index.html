Three questions, one tool

What does this route cost?

Moving gas from Bulgaria through Serbia to Hungary for six months costs about 3.70 euros for every megawatt-hour of gas, with the cheapest mix of products chosen at each border.

BG > Kireevo/Zaychar > RS > Kiskundorozsma 2 > HU, booked 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027, at 400 HUF per EUR: 3.6957 EUR/MWh (euros per megawatt-hour). Booking quarters instead of months saves 0.2956 EUR/MWh.

What will the invoices be?

Booking pipeline capacity at the Austria–Hungary border for October to December means two bills, one from each country’s operator in its own currency; the app shows both and every monthly invoice.

50,000 kWh/d at Mosonmagyaróvár AT>HU for Q4 2026: 2,605.83 EUR to Gas Connect Austria and 782,558 HUF to FGSZ, each split into the three monthly invoices they will send.

How much guarantee do I need?

Eustream, the Slovak operator, asks for a bank guarantee as security for the fees; for this three-month booking it is 280,104.80 euros, worked out from Eustream’s own rule.

100,000 kWh/h at Eustream’s Balassagyarmat SK>HU for Q4 2026, with the gas price index CEGHIX at 30 EUR/MWh, as booked capacity (not at the auction stage): a capacity fee of 361,344.00 EUR, and a guarantee of 280,104.80 EUR.

Why you can trust the numbers

  • The real tariffs of 21 transmission system operators, public on each operator’s own website and collected by hand into one file; routes run across 22 border points between 15 countries.
  • Over 400 automated tests check the app, including every example on this page.
  • Each operator’s own invoicing rule: some bill a quarter as three equal thirds, others by calendar days, and every invoice adds up exactly to its total.
  • The examples use the tariff file of 29 October 2026.

What it does not do (yet)

  • It does not book capacity for you: it tells you what to book and what it will cost; you book on the operator’s platform yourself.
  • It does not fetch tariffs live: the tariff file is updated by hand.
  • It does not choose the path for you: you pick the border points, and it optimises each operator on the route.
  • The guarantee tab knows the rules of Eustream, Gas Connect Austria and FGSZ so far.
  • Exchange rates are typed in, not fetched; no within-day products.
